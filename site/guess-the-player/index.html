Guess the Player

One Tier 1 VCT player, their whole team career laid out at once. You get three guesses.

min teams in career 3

How to play

Every round picks a random Tier 1 player and shows their entire team career at once, oldest team first. You get three guesses to name the player — use them wisely, or hit "give up" to reveal the answer. Use the region and minimum‑teams filters to tune the difficulty: more teams on the timeline means more clues, so it's an easier round.

Data

Full team histories for every player who has appeared in a VCT partnered league (Americas, EMEA, Pacific, China — Kickoff through Stage 2, 2023–2026), scraped from player profile pages on vlr.gg. A handful of teams without listed dates keep their approximate position in the timeline. Stats (streak, played) are stored only in your browser.
