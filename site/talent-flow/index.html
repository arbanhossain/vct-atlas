About this data

What this shows. Every player rostered in a Tier 1 VCT international league — VCT Americas, EMEA, Pacific and China — across the 2023–2026 seasons (Kickoff, Stage 1 and Stage 2), spanning 538 players across 35 league events. A ribbon links a player's nationality to a league if they were rostered on one of that league's teams. Players who competed in more than one region are counted once per league, so the league totals add up to more than the number of unique players.

Source. Compiled from publicly available match and roster data on vlr.gg. Player nationalities, handles and teams reflect vlr.gg profiles at the time of collection (July 2026) and may since have changed.
